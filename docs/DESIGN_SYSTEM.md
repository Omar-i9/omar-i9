# Design System

- Background: deep near-black navy with a centre-weighted cyan, blue, and violet aurora.
- Surfaces: compact translucent glass cards, restrained borders, and readable contrast.
- Type: system sans-serif with short labels and limited uppercase metadata.
- Motion: slow aurora drift, subtle pointer light, fade/blur scene transitions, and desktop-only profile-card repulsion.
- Responsive: 4-column social grid on wide screens, 3 columns on tablets, 2 columns on phones; compact gaming IDs and a stacked TAAMEN card on narrow screens.
- Accessibility: RTL/LTR language switching, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion support, real profile photograph, and descriptive account labels.
